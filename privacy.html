Grox Wallet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Summary

Grox Wallet is a personal money tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Anything you enter (name, email, phone, account and card details, transactions, budgets, settings) is saved only in your device's local app storage. We do not receive, collect, sell or share it. We have no server that holds your data.

Backups

When you save a backup, a file or text containing your data is created on your device. Where it goes after that (Downloads, email, Notes, cloud drive) is up to you. Keep backups private, because they contain all your data. We cannot recover your data if you lose the app and have no backup.

Third-party requests

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when fonts load, under Google's privacy policy. No other third-party services are used.

Not a financial service

Grox Wallet does not move real money and does not connect to banks, cards or payment networks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes your data from the device. You can also use the reset button in Account (hold for 5 seconds).
